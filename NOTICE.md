# Notices

## Third-party plugins

The skills call skills from superpowers, ponytail and ecc. This repo does not include their code. `/ios-orchestration-setup` installs them from their own sources after the user says yes, and each keeps its own licence.

## Company plugins

`mobileapps-ios` and `mobileapps-foundation` come from `gdncomm/agent-plugins`, a private repository. This repo mirrors the `ios-orchestration-*` skills, scripts and hook of that repo, and refers to the other skills by name only.
